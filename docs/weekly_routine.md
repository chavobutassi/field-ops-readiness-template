# Weekly routine (30 minutes)

Written so that a new supervisor can follow it without training. Do it on the same day each week.

1. **Update the data (10 min).** Export the latest records into the five tables. A spreadsheet is enough. Check against `data_dictionary.md` that dates and ids are correct.
2. **Read the readiness table (5 min).** Start with the lowest-scoring area and find its weakest factor.
3. **Clear the backlog (5 min).** Close or reschedule every overdue work order. Assign every unassigned order, preferring a supervisor who covers that area.
4. **Check certificates (3 min).** Renew every certificate that expires within 60 days. Do not assign a job to someone whose required certificate is missing or expired.
5. **Look ahead (4 min).** Before each season, test whether the team can absorb a bad week: pick the worst past month, remove two people, and see which areas lose coverage.
6. **Record the week (3 min).** Write down the readiness index and the five pilot measures (see `pilot_protocol_90_days.md`) and compare them with last week.

## Roles

| Role | Does |
|---|---|
| Operations lead or owner | Reviews the table, decides, signs off the week |
| Supervisor | Updates their area's records, completes their orders' checklists |
| Office staff | Keeps clients, certificates and orders current |
